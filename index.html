About the shop

Azhar Cloth Shop

We are a neighbourhood cloth shop that believes in honest quality and fair rates. Every roll on our shelves is picked with care, so you can sew with confidence whether it is for daily wear, Eid, or a wedding.

Come in, touch the fabric, compare colours in daylight and take your time. Our team will help you work out the right meterage for your suit.

  • Seasonal stock for men, women and kids
  • Help with meterage and fabric matching
  • Friendly, no-pressure service
